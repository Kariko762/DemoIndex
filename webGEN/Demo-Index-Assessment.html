The Demo Index™ Assessment

Purpose: This 15-question assessment evaluates your organization's demo operations maturity across five capability domains.

Time Required: 10 minutes | Output: Demo Index Score (0-100) and Maturity Classification (DI-1 through DI-5)

Instructions

For each question, select the response that best describes your current state. Assign points based on the option selected, then calculate your total score using the weighting provided on page 2.
