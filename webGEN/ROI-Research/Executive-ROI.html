Platform Maintenance & Governance (5 – 15 Hours/Month Per Demo Track)

To keep interactive demos accurate and aligned with our main product updates, the platform uses a structured monthly and quarterly maintenance routine:

  • Monthly UI Update Checks: Reviewing active demos after product software releases. If our main app design or button layouts change, we update the interactive click points so the self-serve paths remain seamless.
  • Links & Analytics Audits: Verifying all outbound web links and CRM tracking metrics. This guarantees that customer engagement data continually flows into sales dashboards without interruption.
  • Quarterly Feature Refreshes: Retiring outdated product screens. We swap in fresh screenshots and new user flows to accurately reflect our latest product capabilities.
  • User Access & Messaging Reviews: Cleaning up user seats to remove inactive accounts, while standardizing our demo template library to ensure Sales and Marketing stay completely aligned.
