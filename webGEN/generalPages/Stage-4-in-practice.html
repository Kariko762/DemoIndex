How The Stack Connects

Salesforce → Coast.io

API Integration: Coast.io pulls account data, deal stage, persona attributes, and custom fields from Salesforce via REST API. Webhook Triggers: When deal moves to "Qualified," Coast.io auto-generates base experience template. Bi-directional sync: Engagement analytics push back to Salesforce activity timeline.

Coast.io → Synthesia

Dynamic Script Generation: Coast.io generates video scripts with account-specific context, persona messaging, and narrative framing. API Rendering: Synthesia renders AI video with injected data, industry-specific language, and buyer persona avatars. Embedded Delivery: Video embeds directly in Coast.io unified experience with contextual branching.

How Governance Is Maintained

Pre-Structured Pathways

SEs define allowed exploration branches in Coast.io. Prospects can navigate nonlinearly, but all paths are pre-authored with contextual bridges. No chaotic free-form exploration. Human-guided narrative branching.

Messaging Consistency

All video scripts, UI copy, and guidance messages follow approved templates. Synthesia generates variations within guardrails. RevOps teams control positioning, competitive framing, and value prop emphasis. Brand-safe AI personalization.

Persistent Contextual State

Coast.io maintains journey state across all touchpoints. If prospect jumps from Integrations → Analytics → Security, AI guidance adjusts to maintain narrative thread. No cognitive resets.

Full Journey Analytics

Track narrative comprehension, not just clicks. See where buyer understanding accelerated, where narrative confidence weakened, which contextual pathways produced strongest engagement. Intelligence-grade analytics.

Why This Is Stage 4 (Not Stage 3 with Better Tools)

Stage 3 companies have Salesforce, demo tools, and AI video — but they operate as disconnected point solutions. Stage 4 is the architectural shift to Unified Narrative Infrastructure where: (1) Humans retain strategic authority over positioning and narrative design, (2) Systems operationalize delivery through data injection, personalization, and assembly, (3) Persistent contextual continuity is maintained across all exploration paths, and (4) Governance rules ensure brand-safe, controlled experiences. This is orchestration infrastructure, not better demos.
