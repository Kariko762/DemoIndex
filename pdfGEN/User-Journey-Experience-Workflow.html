Stage 4 User Journey Experience Workflow

Granular operating workflow showing what happens at each step, who engages, which systems are involved, what is automated versus human-controlled, and which glossary capabilities are active.

This workflow assumes a Stage 4 production environment only. Humans define strategy, approved branches, and guardrails. Connected systems operationalize assembly, guidance, contextual continuity, analytics, and seller activation.

Reference Stack and Operating Model

Reference Stack

  • CRM and account system: Salesforce
  • Marketing automation: HubSpot or Marketo
  • Orchestration platform: Coast.io-style orchestration layer or custom orchestration app
  • Presentation layer: Seismic Interactive Content, Seismic Microsites, Coast.io Engagement Portal, or custom React/Next.js
  • CMS and content model: Contentful, Sanity, or structured content service
  • Event collection: Segment, RudderStack, or Snowplow
  • AI gateway service: internal AI service with prompt orchestration and policy controls
  • LLM: Gemini via Vertex AI or Google AI Studio API

Operating Roles and Automation Model

  • AE: owns account strategy, progression, and seller follow-up
  • SE: defines narrative, workflow emphasis, objections, and approved paths
  • RevOps: governs templates, measurement, routing rules, and CRM integration
  • Human Orchestrated: a person explicitly decides strategy, pathway, or priority
  • Human in the Loop: a person approves, edits, or overrides machine output
  • Automated: the system executes from rules, triggers, or services
  • Intelligence Assisted: the system suggests options inside human-defined boundaries

Human-Orchestrated Versus Automated Summary

  • Narrative strategy: humans set value proposition, narrative hierarchy, workflow priority, and objection framing; AI suggestions remain reviewable.
  • Experience assembly: humans choose allowed branches and priorities; systems handle presentation-layer assembly, data injection, URL creation, and module loading.
  • Guidance generation: humans define policy boundaries; systems handle prompt building, retrieval, Gemini generation, and runtime rendering, with review on high-risk outputs.
  • Runtime navigation: buyers choose paths; systems handle state updates, bridge injection, recommendation, and analytics capture; sellers respond to high-value alerts.
  • Post-session optimization: humans define KPIs and governance thresholds; systems handle scoring, summarization, and CRM writeback, while AEs review follow-up content.

Practical Reading

Stage 4 is human-led orchestration with system-level execution. Humans still define strategy, approved branches, messaging guardrails, and escalation paths, while the platform automates assembly, guidance presentation, state updates, analytics capture, and seller activation. Even when Gemini is used inside Stage 4, it operates within human-defined prompts, approved content boundaries, retrieval filters, and policy checks. AI supports delivery and explanation. It does not independently set journey strategy.
