Where is FIS?

FIS is moving through a clear four-step transformation: from fragmented tooling, to enterprise agreement and scale, to integration orchestration, and then to AI-led proactive asset creation.

Fragmented Baseline

Today, the core capabilities are available through separate tool licenses and disconnected workflows. Teams can execute, but orchestration, shared standards, and end-to-end visibility are still fragmented across the operating model.

Enterprise Scale

The immediate move is consolidating into an enterprise agreement so Demo Intelligence and Demo Automation can scale with consistent governance, broader adoption, and a shared operating foundation across regions and teams.

Integration Orchestration

Once scaled, the focus shifts to orchestration: integrating systems, data, and workflows so experiences are connected, context-aware, and operationally coordinated instead of executed as standalone activities.

AI-Led Governance

After orchestration is in place, AI-led governance determines which demos should be created and when buyer engagement should move to human interaction, with human-in-the-loop approval governing every high-impact decision.
