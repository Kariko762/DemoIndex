# HOMEPAGE STRATEGY
## The Demo Index
### Executive Framework for Revenue Experience Transformation

---

# PURPOSE OF THE HOMEPAGE

The homepage should not introduce *The Demo Index*.

It should introduce **the business problem**.

Executives are not asking:

> "What is The Demo Index?"

They are asking:

- Why is Demo changing?
- Why does this matter to our business?
- What risks do we face if we don't adapt?
- How mature is our organisation?
- What should we invest in next?

The Demo Index is simply the framework that answers those questions.

---

# EXECUTIVE NARRATIVE

The homepage should tell a story.

Rather than presenting documentation immediately, it should establish context before introducing the framework.

The journey should be:

1. The market is changing.
2. Revenue Experience is becoming a competitive differentiator.
3. Traditional demo operations are no longer sufficient.
4. Organisations require a structured maturity model.
5. The Demo Index provides that model.

---

# HERO SECTION

## Revenue Experience Is Becoming a Competitive Advantage

Modern enterprise buyers expect far more than traditional product demonstrations.

Artificial Intelligence, personalised buyer experiences, self-service demonstrations, digital sales rooms, and automated content generation are fundamentally changing how enterprise organisations engage prospects throughout the buying journey.

Most organisations continue to measure demo success using methodologies designed for a pre-AI sales environment.

The Demo Index provides executive leadership with a structured framework to understand current capability, benchmark organisational maturity, and identify strategic investment priorities for the future.

# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The homepage should feel less like a public website and more like an executive strategy portal.

Avoid language such as:

- Document 1
- Document 2
- Download PDF

Instead use:

- Executive Framework
- Capability Model
- Executive Assessment
- Research Library
- Strategic Roadmap

Every section should answer one executive question before introducing the next.

The homepage should establish credibility, explain why the framework exists, and create a compelling narrative that encourages leaders to begin the assessment journey.
